Privacy

Advent Bible Journey stores your language, completed days, onboarding state and optional reminder settings on your device. No account is required.

The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, chat, user uploads, purchases or an application server. We do not receive your reading progress.

Stories and illustrations are bundled for offline reading. Optional local reminders require notification permission; they do not require a push token or a remote notification service. No camera, microphone, contacts or location access is requested.

External YouTube and map links are behind a parental gate. Opening them sends you to a third-party service with its own privacy practices. The app does not supply your reading progress to those services.

Use Settings → Reset Advent journey to erase completed days. Uninstalling removes the app’s local data, subject to operating-system backup/restore behavior. Language and reminder settings are not removed by resetting progress.
